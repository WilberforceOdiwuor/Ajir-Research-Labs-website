Focus areas

We march toward the revival of human epistemic independence through AI, by researching and developing agentic and multimodal systems and deploying them ethically.

The work

AI that makes people think better

Our AI research starts with a problem most benchmarks miss: cognitive offloading. When memory, reasoning, and judgment are handed to LLMs, thinking does not just get faster — it gets weaker. We study that cost, and design against it.

Then we build the alternative. Agentic, multimodal systems that show their reasoning, keep uncertainty visible, and stay grounded in the content in front of you. AskNoema applies this to short-form media, where misinformation travels faster than anyone can fact-check it.

One rule governs everything we ship: human dignity comes first. We publish our findings openly and build to the AI governance standards that matter — so augmentation strengthens independent judgment instead of quietly replacing it.

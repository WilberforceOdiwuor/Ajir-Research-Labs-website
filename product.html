AskNoema

Verify what you watch, as you watch it

The platform where you research and verify your own feed. Multi-agent AI checks the claims inside social media and trending news in real time — without ever pulling you out of the stream.

The problem

The future of social networks is social intelligence

AskNoema is built for active feed research — powered by multi-agent AI that verifies media claims and trending news in real time.

Our solution

Verification that travels with the content

Instead of cross-checking tabs or falling for online misinformation, AskNoema lets you consume trending social content alongside verified, instant multi-agent AI claim breakdowns, source verification, and deep research.

Capabilities

Three things the feed has never done

Context-locked overlay

Context-locked AI overlay and real-time social intelligence

Never get misled by a viral clip again. AskNoema analyzes content in real time, delivering instant source ratings, verified facts, and context-locked analysis without ever leaving your stream.

Research dossiers

Topic deep dives and interactive research dossiers

Instant academic depth for every video topic. Convert brief video mentions into cited, structured research dossiers. Explore primary sources, counter-arguments, and deep context without ever leaving your active session.

Source lineage

Source lineage and fact-checking

Trace every online claim back to its primary origin. Eliminate noise and unsourced opinion. AskNoema maps creator claims against real-time web indexes, peer-reviewed literature, and primary data streams — giving you full visibility into where facts come from.

The workflow

multi-agent workflow system between media and AI analysis output

Each subagent deploys its output to the next, emulating the multi-step verification system on verified media houses.

01 / 09

media.intake L0 · Boundary

Media intake

The upload media is demuxed into an audio track, a sampled frame set and located text regions.

In
upload
Out
demuxed streams

asr.transcription L2 · Extraction

Transcription

Transcription agents runs to retrieve timestamped verbose data from uploaded media.

In
16 kHz mono PCM
Out
segments + word timings

vision.recognition L3 · Extraction

Vision annotation

Vision agents describes what each frame shows — subject, setting, figures on screen, documents held up to camera.

In
media frame set
Out
per-frame annotations

ocr.analysis L3 · Extraction

OCR · analysis

OCR systems reads the located regions verbatim, and catches exact wording that a vision models paraphrase.

In
text regions
Out
literal strings

Data Assortment L3 · Contract

Episteme Proprietary Agent

Transcript, annotations and OCR merge into a single contract-typed artifact, and every later stage reads and writes here rather than touching raw media. It is also where resolved claims are cached by fingerprint.

In
extractor outputs
Out
one typed artifact

claim.extraction L4 · Reasoning

Claim extraction

Agent separates propositions from everything else. Rhetoric, framing, opinion and jokes are set aside so the verifier only ever sees statements that can actually be true or false. Most of a clip is not a claim.

In
record
Out
checkable claims

web.verification L5 · Evidence

Web verification

Each claim goes to the open web with its own query set. Sources are weighed rather than counted — one primary document can outrank ten aggregators repeating each other, and a single source can be split.

In
claim set
Out
weighed sources

synthesis L6 · Assembly

Synthesis

Evidence is reconciled into a verdict and a veracity score. Disagreement is preserved rather than averaged away: where credible sources genuinely conflict, the output says contested and shows the split.

In
record + evidence
Out
verdict + veracity

display L7 · Assembly

Display

The response is content-locked to the video and timestamp it was asked about, then rendered. Nothing here is computed on the client — verdict, score and sources all arrive from the runtime.

In
response object
Out
feed surface
